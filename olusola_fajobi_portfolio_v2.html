About

My story

A journey from the clinic to the command centre of population health intelligence

01
Clinician & researcher
Trained as a physician at Obafemi Awolowo University, I saw the chasm between patient data and clinical decisions — driving me into public health. I earned my MPH, MWACP and FWACP, publishing 19 peer-reviewed papers spanning COVID-19, reproductive health, FGM and NCDs.
02
Population health analyst
As Senior Population Health Analyst at NHS Mid and South Essex ICB, I built analytical systems linking datasets of over one million pseudonymised records — using R, SQL and Power BI to surface health inequalities and inform commissioning decisions across the integrated care system.
03
Intelligence leader
Now leading the Public Health Intelligence function at Thurrock Council — managing a team of analysts, delivering automated dashboards with smart narration, ML risk stratification models, and reproducible analytical pipelines that directly shape public health strategy.

Philosophy

Leadership vision

What drives my approach to public health intelligence

"
Good data alone does not improve health. It is the people who understand both the data and the communities it represents who drive real change.
My career has been defined by a belief that clinical understanding and analytical rigour must work together. Having trained as a physician, conducted field research across Nigeria, and now leading intelligence systems that serve over a million people in Essex, I know that the most powerful insights sit at the intersection of domain expertise and technical capability. As a leader, I aim to build teams that are curious, reproducible in their methods, and relentless in translating evidence into action — particularly for populations historically underserved by health systems. The future of public health lies in AI-augmented intelligence: faster, fairer, and grounded in the communities it serves.
